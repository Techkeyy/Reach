## Judging

The official rules list the following criteria. No numeric weights were found in the inspected official sources.

| Criterion | Status | What Reach must evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Functionality | VERIFIED | Real create, fund, claim, expiry, reclaim, reconciliation, and failure-path evidence |
| Potential Impact | VERIFIED | A clear problem for senders, businesses, and recipients who are blocked by wallet collection |
| Novelty | VERIFIED | Walletless recipient onboarding connected to a real escrow entitlement, not a fake payment link |
| UX | VERIFIED | Value-first recipient flow, mobile claim page, sponsored-fee path where proven, human status language |
| Open-source | VERIFIED | Public repository if possible, documented primitives, tests, limitations, and composition surface |
| Business Plan | VERIFIED | API and business payout wedge, operational model, and a credible path beyond a demo |

The official FAQ also describes founder and market fit, insight, product and execution, potential market size, founder communication, viability, and traction as evaluation factors. These are not presented as numeric weights in the source.
